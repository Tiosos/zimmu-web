# Stage 2 — room geometry and placement notes

2026-09-29: Version 22 stores optional local XY room geometry on each Room. Existing v21 files remain readable; their rooms have no geometry until edited. A converted file first saves as a new copy. The scene remains the geometry authority for cabinets, and existing cabinet-to-cabinet anchors still name components rather than walls.

Walls are directed line segments in a room-local frame, transformed by origin and rotation for the plan. An opening belongs to one wall by stable ID and uses an offset measured from that wall's start. Obstacles are local rectangles with height. A site's measured wall length is separate from the drawn length, with source, timestamp and uncertainty. The editor requires an explicitly entered measured value, source and uncertainty; it never copies the drawn length and labels it as a site measurement.

A wall placement names a cabinet and wall, longitudinal offset, inward setback and accepted manual tangent/normal offsets. Editing the wall or room frame recomputes its cabinet's scene transform as a derived update without inserting an undo entry that would immediately be reapplied. Project structure undo/redo changes the placement constraint; unlinking leaves the cabinet at its current world pose. Dragging or turning the cabinet in plan or 3D removes that wall placement and leaves it free. Deleting a cabinet or moving its ownership to another room prunes its placement. This stage does not reinterpret `Anchor.to` as a wall reference.

The kitchenette fixture supplies an approximate 3983 mm straight run; it is not a site-verified as-built record. The synthetic two-wall test exercises a rotated return. Geometry warnings identify unverified lengths, drawn/measured differences outside the stated uncertainty, openings beyond wall ends, placement offsets beyond wall ends, and possible cabinet/obstacle footprint overlap. The latter uses a conservative envelope for rotated obstacles; it is a prompt for designer review, not a production-release check.

Follow-up within Stage 2: dimensioned wall elevations, door swing/drawer clearance, floor/site level evidence, and accepted offset behavior for a constrained chain of cabinets need separate geometry rules and review. Do not claim these warnings certify a room or clear a production release.
