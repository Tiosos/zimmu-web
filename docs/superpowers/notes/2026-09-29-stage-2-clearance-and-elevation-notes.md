# Stage 2 — site levels, elevations and clearances

2026-09-29: V23 keeps the v22 room and scene representation. Optional signed site-level readings are room-local points relative to a named project datum. Each reading requires a source, timestamp and uncertainty; no level is inferred from a cabinet's Z coordinate. Old v22 rooms without this evidence load unchanged and first save as a new copy.

Wall elevations show each wall's drawn length, the offset, width, sill and height of its openings, and projected width/height of cabinets explicitly linked to that wall. They use the same room transform and current scene as the plan. They are design views, not as-built surveys or formal shop sheets; a missing site reading remains visible.

Operable cabinet fronts derive from the section tree or independent face-frame openings. Each physical opening requires a designer-entered projection distance before clearance can be assessed. The checker builds a conservative full-opening footprint in front of a door or drawer, composes nested cabinet transforms, tests its vertical interval, and flags possible overlap with room obstacles or other cabinets owned by that room. A room door likewise requires explicit hinge end, swing side and reach; its square sweep envelope is conservative rather than a precise arc. If entered reach is shorter than door width, the envelope uses the width and warns about the mismatch. No hinge angle, hardware travel or compliance clearance is invented. Unassessed fronts and room doors remain warnings.

The kitchenette's approximately 3983 mm wall is an illustrative, unverified design length. Tests also cover a synthetic two-wall return, rotated cabinet front, site-level round-trip and obstruction heights. Clearance warnings guide design review; they do not certify access, building-code compliance, as-built dimensions or production release. Door arcs, complex obstacles and required accessibility clearances need project-specific verified rules before any automated approval gate.
