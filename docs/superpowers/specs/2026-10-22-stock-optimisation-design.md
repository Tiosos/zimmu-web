# Sheet stock and offcut optimisation

Build the selected 50-task batch on a46d820, preserving the prior unmerged grain work. Scene.stockPlan holds versioned project-local inventory and job settings; no shared inventory server or automatic purchasing. Sheets have stable IDs, names, material/thickness, dimensions, grain along length/width/none, finite quantity or unlimited purchases, price, trim, enabled flag and location. Offcuts require finite quantities. Selection is saved as explicit cabinet IDs (empty selects all boards, including manual boards); room filtering changes selection explicitly.

Use canonical manufacturing records, actual cut bounds and derived matched bindings. The new mixed-stock solver conservatively reserves rectangular footprints, including shape bounding boxes; it does not reuse holes or claim guillotine/CNC feasibility. Matched groups remain atomic and must be complete. A deterministic guillotine free-rectangle partition reserves isotropic clearance and trim, tests allowed rotations against stock grain, opens existing bins before new stock, respects quantities and ranks candidate stock by strategy. Compare deterministic heuristics: fewer sheets (most demand area packed on the candidate), lower immediate purchase cost per packed area, and offcut first. These are estimates, not global optima.

Bound stock/job counts and dimensions, validate before computation, never allocate raster sheets. Derive disjoint rectangular free remnants, excluding edge trim and clearance; apply minimum dimensions. Preview all strategies against current single-size stock baseline. Show stock identities, real part labels, group outlines, source coordinates, counts, costs, coverage, waste, unplaced reasons, remaining quantities and proposed remnants.

Acceptance checks exact scene/project/effective-material signature and recomputes from the draft. Save settings is one scene command. Explicit consume action requires complete allocation, consumes finite inventory and optionally creates selected remnants using unique IDs, all in one undo step. It neither changes cabinet geometry nor runs on preview/save/export. Stale previews cannot export or apply. Unlimited purchased stock remains unlimited. Format 41 validates and preserves optional data; older files remain supported. CSV and SVG exports include allocations, stock purchase counts and reusable remnants.

No new dependencies or agents.
